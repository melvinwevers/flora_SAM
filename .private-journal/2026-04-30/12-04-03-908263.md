## Project Notes

## Flora color analysis: discovering the fundamental issue

Spent hours trying different chroma threshold approaches (15→8, post-filter, oversampling, etc) but kept missing pink/blue flowers.

**Root cause discovered**: K-means with 100 clusters DOES find the pink/blue pixels, but they have moderate chroma (12-16) while the dominant green leaves have higher chroma (20-30). Sorting purely by chroma means rare but visually interesting colors (pink flowers) get filtered out.

**Example**: Plant KW_T_423_12_0024L has pink pixels at 0.46% with chroma 14.58, but green leaves dominate with chroma 20-25. Top-20-by-chroma returns 20 different shades of yellow-green.

**Solution needed**: Color diversity, not just chroma. Need to ensure we sample from different hue regions (green, pink, blue, yellow) even if some have lower chroma. This is a ranking/selection problem, not a clustering problem.

**Lesson**: When debugging data extraction, check BOTH "did we find it?" and "did we keep it after filtering?". I found the colors but filtered them out.
